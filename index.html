Final-year group project, two semesters

Thywill Electrical Services: job and inventory management

A system for an electrical and construction business to manage customers, jobs, technicians and stock. It started as a Windows Forms desktop app and was extended into an ASP.NET Web Forms version with a layered architecture.

What I did: worked on the desktop app's service and data-access layers, CRUD and validation for customers, jobs, staff and inventory, job workflows (technician assignment, status, stock needs), and cost calculations and PDF invoices.

The database ran on a university-controlled SQL Server, so anything that depends on it is limited by that environment.

Group project, COMP315

Concurrent inventory and trading system

A C++ console application that processes 20 orders across 9 warehouses at the same time. Mutexes protect shared stock so orders are rejected cleanly when inventory runs out.

What I did: the mutex-based synchronisation around inventory updates, shared ownership with std::shared_ptr, and the CLI menu and handler functions.

  • C++
  • std::thread
  • std::mutex
  • Smart pointers
